1. Executive Summary & System Purpose

smol café is a state-of-the-art, real-time café operating system and customer dining application. Built with Next.js 15 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS, it bridges customer self-ordering, front-desk cashier settlement, chef/barista kitchen ticket routing, and owner analytics into a unified real-time synchronization loop.

⚡ Real-Time Synchronous Engine (lib/sync-events.ts)

Unlike traditional multi-page apps that require frequent polling or refreshes, smol café utilizes an event-driven broadcast channel architecture. Whenever an order is submitted, verified, pushed to kitchen, or marked ready, events trigger instant UI transitions across all active tabs and devices in under 50ms.

4. Customer Dining Journey Flow (Patron Dining)

1
Table Discovery & Session Binding (/t/table-01 ... table-12)

Guest arrives and scans the cryptographic QR code on their dining table. The URL binds the user's browser session directly to that specific table and seating zone (e.g. Indoor Cozy, Courtyard Verandah, Garden Terrace, Brew Bar).

2
Menu Browsing & Pure-Code Daily Blackboard (/home, /menu)

Customers browse curated specialty coffees, sourdough melts, fresh bakes, and teas. The homepage features a dynamic, pure-code chalkboard rendered with Caveat handwritten typography showing the daily single-origin roast and recommendation:

today's special: Ratnagiri Estate Pour Over with warm hazelnut notes ♡
— fresh morning bake: cinnamon almond bun ready at 8:00 AM
3
Item Customization & Dietary Precision

Clicking any item opens the Customization Drawer: Oat Milk vs Almond Milk vs Whole Milk, sweetness sliders (0%, 50%, 100%), temperature (Hot vs Iced), and barista notes.

4
Order Submission & 4-Digit Table Verification PIN (/cart)

When the customer submits their cart, the system assigns a unique Order ID and displays a 4-digit Table Verification PIN. This prevents prank orders from going to the kitchen until verified by the cashier.

5
Live Status Bar & Dynamic UPI Bill Payment (/order/[id], /bill)

Customers follow the live progress bar: Order Placed ➔ PIN Verified ➔ Brewing/Cooking ➔ Ready to Serve. Upon finishing dining, guest requests digital bill, generates a dynamic UPI deep-link QR (upi://pay?pa=...), and receives an itemized digital chit with 5% GST calculated.

5. Cashier Desk Front-Line Operations (/cashier)

Tab 1: Verification Queue

Incoming orders appear with an amber halo. The cashier asks the patron for their 4-digit PIN, verifies it against the screen, and clicks "Confirm Order". The chit is instantly broadcast to the kitchen.

Tab 2: Tables & Settlement

A visual overview of all 12 tables. Cashier can open tables for walk-in guests, view accumulated round totals, launch UPI QR on the counter display, or record Cash payment with automatic change-due calculator.

Tab 3: Paid Orders & Audit Ledger

Maintains today's complete payment audit trail: Settlement ID, table number, tender method (PhonePe, Google Pay, Cash), exact timestamps, and a button to re-print or view digital guest chits.

7. Admin Control Tower (/admin)

Protected by an encrypted passcode gate (Demo PIN: 8888), the Admin Tower is the café manager's cockpit:

📊 Live Sales & Demand Analytics

Real-time daily gross revenue, orders count, table occupancy %, average ticket size (₹384.50), peak demand curve by hour (Peak 5PM: 26 orders/hr), and bestseller rankings.

🪑 12 Tables Floor Map & JSON Tags

Interactive floor plan displaying table status (Free vs Busy). Generates encrypted Table JSON Tags and dynamic QR bill links for any dining table.

📦 59-Item Menu Stock Toggle

One-click toggling between IN STOCK and SOLD OUT for all coffees, sandwiches, and treats. Instantly disables sold-out items on active customer devices.

🎖️ Smol Club Loyalty & Merchant Setup

Patron point ledger (₹10 spent = 1 pt). Tier perks: Seedling (0-100 pts), Regular (101-500 pts), Smol Insider (500+ pts). Configures merchant UPI VPA and 5% GST rate.
