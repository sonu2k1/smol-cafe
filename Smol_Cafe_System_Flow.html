1. Executive Summary & System Purpose

smol café is a state-of-the-art, real-time café operating system and customer dining application. Built with Next.js 15 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS, it bridges customer self-ordering, front-desk cashier settlement, dedicated barista espresso shots, kitchen KDS dispatch, and super-admin inventory radar into a unified zero-latency synchronization loop.

⚡ Real-Time Synchronous Engine (lib/sync-events.ts)

Unlike traditional multi-page apps that require frequent polling or refreshes, smol café utilizes an event-driven broadcast channel & Supabase Realtime mesh. Whenever an order is submitted, verified, pushed to barista/kitchen, restocked, or settled, events trigger instant UI transitions across all active tabs and devices in under 50ms.

4. Customer Dining Journey Flow (Patron Dining)

1
Table Discovery & Session Binding (/t/table-01 ... table-12)

Guest arrives and scans the cryptographic QR code on their dining table. The URL binds the user's browser session directly to that specific table and seating zone (e.g. Indoor Cozy, Courtyard Verandah, Garden Terrace, Brew Bar).

2
Menu Browsing & Pure-Code Daily Blackboard (/home, /menu)

Customers browse curated specialty coffees, sourdough melts, fresh bakes, and teas. The homepage features a dynamic, pure-code chalkboard rendered with Caveat handwritten typography showing the daily single-origin roast and recommendation:

today's special: Ratnagiri Estate Pour Over with warm hazelnut notes ♡
— fresh morning bake: cinnamon almond bun ready at 8:00 AM
3
Item Customization & Dietary Precision

Clicking any item opens the Customization Drawer: Oat Milk vs Almond Milk vs Whole Milk, sweetness sliders (0%, 50%, 100%), temperature (Hot vs Iced), and barista notes.

4
Phone-Number Loyalty & Order Submission (/cart)

Customers enter their 10-digit phone number to track visits (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 5th visit bonuses) and redeem Smol Club reward points for instant rupee discounts. When the cart is submitted, the system assigns a unique Order ID and displays a 4-digit Table Verification PIN.

5
Live Status Bar & Dynamic UPI Bill Payment (/order/[id], /bill)

Customers follow the live progress bar: Order Placed ➔ PIN Verified ➔ Brewing/Cooking ➔ Ready to Serve. Upon finishing dining, guest requests digital bill, generates a dynamic UPI deep-link QR (upi://pay?pa=...), and receives an itemized thermal slip with 5% GST calculated.

5. Cashier Desk Front-Line Operations (/cashier)

Tab 1: Verification Queue

Incoming orders appear with an amber halo. The cashier asks the patron for their 4-digit PIN, verifies it against the screen, and clicks "Confirm Order". The chit is instantly broadcast to barista and kitchen.

Tab 2: Tables & Settlement

A visual overview of all 12 tables. Cashier can open tables for walk-in guests, view accumulated round totals, launch UPI QR on the counter display, or record Cash payment with automatic change-due calculator.

Tab 3: Paid Orders & Printable Invoices

Maintains today's complete payment audit trail: Settlement ID, table number, tender method (UPI, Google Pay, Cash), exact timestamps, and a button to re-print 58mm/80mm thermal guest chits.
